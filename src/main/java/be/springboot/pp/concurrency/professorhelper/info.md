## Problem statement
A professor teaches two batches A and B. He provides consultation to 3 students at a time. All can only be from the same batch. After consulting a slot 10 students, professor takes a break and then again consults 10 more, which goes on.

Professor wants to be fair, if in last 4 slots same batch students are served then next slot will be reserved for another batch.
